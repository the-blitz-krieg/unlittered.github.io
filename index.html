Origin Story

Not Just Another Consultant

I did not learn business in a classroom. I learned it in a garage, at age 13, manufacturing window blinds, importing components directly from Taiwan and China via Alibaba, and building real client relationships before most of my peers had their first summer job.

When the recession of 2008 hit and the blinds business took losses, I did not fold. I pivoted. I taught myself copywriting, landed my first client, and spent the next 15 years climbing from Content Writer to Director of Digital Marketing, then to VP of Marketing and Operations, and eventually founding multiple ventures of my own.

Today I sit at the intersection of entrepreneurship, digital marketing, and analytics, helping companies navigate digital transformation with both creativity and rigour. My edge is this: I have been on every side of the table, as founder, operator, marketer, builder, and advisor.
